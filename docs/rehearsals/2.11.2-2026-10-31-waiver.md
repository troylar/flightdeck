# Two-computer rehearsal waived (not rehearsed): Flightdeck 2.11.2

- Version: 2.11.2
- Date: 2026-10-31
- Decision: the project owner waived the two-computer rehearsal (docs/rehearsals/README.md, *The release gate*) for this release.

## Why it was asked for

2.11.2 is a patch, so the gate doesn't require a rehearsal. It changes when a window accepts the answer that says its identity is taken, and that identity decides who counts as recording a run.

## What the rehearsal would have covered

The script's steps about one computer with two windows open on a run. Duplicating a tab isn't a two-computer step, so most of the script doesn't apply.

## What covers it instead

Automated tests in `./test/run.sh`: the R11 rules in `test/app.test.js`, with the first window's answer held until before and after the 300 ms wait, plus reloads keeping their identity. `mutateRun`, `canReplace`, `recover`, `applyRunRead`, `useFs`, `acceptProposal` and `queued` are byte for byte as in 2.11.1.

## What isn't proven on real OneDrive or in a real browser

- A real browser's duplicated tab: automation can't press Duplicate tab, so the tests use a stand-in for the channel and a copied session.
