# Two-computer rehearsal waived (not rehearsed): Flightdeck 2.10.0

- Version: 2.10.0
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Decision: the project owner waived the two-computer rehearsal (docs/rehearsals/README.md, *The release gate*) for this release.

## Why it was required

2.10.0 changes no file format and writes nothing new to the team folder: the Markdown a checklist is saved as is byte for byte what 2.9.0 writes for the same edit. It does change when typing in the editor counts as pending, in `src/ui/app.js`: a box filled by Insert a detail or a picked suggestion now applies when it's left, and a refused box becomes the pending one. Pending typing decides whether a newer draft arriving from the folder, or a proposal from an AI assistant, may replace what's on screen. So this isn't a release with no sync change, and by the rule in docs/rehearsals/README.md it needs the rehearsal.

## What the rehearsal would have covered

The script's steps and the accounting tally, run once with both computers on 2.10.0 and once with one on 2.9.0, on a test folder holding:

- a draft open on computer A with a picked suggestion not yet applied while computer B saves a newer copy of the same draft (A keeps its typing and takes the newer copy only once the box is applied or discarded);
- a proposal from an AI assistant arriving while a box holds typing (it waits; it never replaces the box);
- a checklist edited in the new step form on 2.10.0 and opened on 2.9.0 (it reads the same).

## What covers it instead

Automated tests in `./test/run.sh`, all green on the release commit:

- **Typing that isn't applied**: `test/guide-editor.test.js` (a picked suggestion and Insert a detail, each applied once on leaving, with one Undo entry) and `test/app.test.js` (typing held through a newer folder draft, live apply, Undo and selection; the refusal shown beside its box with Apply and Discard; Discarding one of two boxes leaves the other blocking).
- **Incoming drafts and proposals**: `test/live-app.test.js` and the app tests' paused races.
- **The same Markdown as before**: `test/clarity-compat.test.js` (the same edits made in the 2.9.0 app as it shipped and in 2.10.0 save the same Markdown, byte for byte) and `test/app.test.js` (Must be done and Automation planned write the marks the 2.9.0 Kind choice wrote, with Undo, Redo and a reload).
- **Older copies, from their own shipped code**: `test/compat.test.js`, `test/compat-bridge.test.js` and `test/old-bundles.test.js`, with 2.9.0 now recorded as an older release.

Also run on the release commit: browser checks in headless Chromium (26 of 26: focus after each new control, the refusal beside its box with Apply and Discard, More options with typing, the phone sheet's 44px targets and Close, Escape and Back), two walkthroughs of a first-time coordinator and developer, and three rounds of code review until nothing was left (round 1: four findings, round 2: one, all fixed with tests).

## What isn't proven on real OneDrive

- A newer draft arriving over real sync delays while a box holds a picked suggestion that isn't applied.
- A mixed team (2.9.0 and 2.10.0) editing the same draft over real sync delays.

## What still runs by hand

The one-computer browser check in Edge and Chrome from a real `file://` path: pick a detail from the suggestions and leave the box; open More options with typing in a folded box; add a role from a step; start a checklist with an approval.
