## Before planning · DO-CONFIRM
- [!] Last sprint closed in Jira
- [ ] Unfinished stories moved or sent back to the backlog
- [ ] Time off for the next two weeks entered as capacity
- [~] Sprint dates copied into the team roadmap

## Planning · READ-DO
- [!] Sprint goal written as one sentence everyone can repeat
- [!] Every committed story has acceptance criteria
  > Use the story template: intent, "done when", expected impact. A story without "done when" goes back to refinement.
- [ ] Dependencies on other teams named in the story
- [ ] Committed points at or under capacity

## After planning · DO-CONFIRM
- [!] Sprint started in Jira ^sprint
- [ ] Goal posted in the team channel
- [~] Sprint board link added to the channel topic
